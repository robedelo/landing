Questions, answered

The essentials before we start.

How long does a website take? +

A focused landing page can usually be completed in a few weeks. Larger sites depend on their scope, content, and feedback cycle. At the start, we will agree a simple timeline with the points where I need your feedback.

What determines the final price? +

The scope, number of pages, integrations, content support, and any custom functionality determine the final quote. You will have a clear proposal before work begins, so you know what is included.

Do I need to have every text and photo ready? +

Not necessarily. You can bring what you already have, and we will create a clear structure for anything missing. Support with defining the website messaging can also be part of the project.

What if I need something outside the plans? +

The plans are a guide, not a limit. If you need an integration, calculator, private area, or a specific feature, we will review the idea and prepare a tailored proposal.

Will I need maintenance after launch? +

It depends on the technology and how your business develops. We can build a simple, low-maintenance site or agree ongoing support for improvements, updates, and new needs.

Will the website really be mine? +

Yes. The aim is for you to have clarity over what is delivered and control over your digital presence. Before launch, we will review access, tools, and management options with you.
